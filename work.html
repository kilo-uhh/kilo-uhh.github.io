Our work

Community water portal for Hawaiʻi Island

An Earth observation data portal built in partnership with the Division of Aquatic Resources, curating products for turbidity, land disturbance, and benthic habitat relevant to Hawaiʻi Island.

turbidityland disturbance benthic habitatdata curation

STELLA for aquatic remote sensing

We adapt NASA's open-hardware STELLA spectrometer for aquatic observation, sourcing sustainable materials suited to islands and partnering with K-12 schools.
