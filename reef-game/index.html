How to play

You are the DAR reef response office, caring for 10 coral restoration sites around Hawaiʻi Island. Events arrive by chance. Respond to each one and leave the reef stronger for the next. If 4 sites are lost, the whole office loses.

Teams and sites

  • Bleaching Response owns the Shallow sites, hit by marine heatwaves.
  • Storm Response owns the Exposed sites, hit by swells, Kona low and hurricanes.
  • Land-Based Pollution Response owns the Runoff sites, hit by floods and storm rain.
  • Puakō has all three traits and is shared by every team.

Each round

  1. Draw: an event card is revealed.
  2. Huddle: your team decides what to ask for and picks a rep.
  3. Office meeting: reps divide the budget and must reach consensus. Default: 6 tokens to the team hit, 1 to each other team. No consensus means the default applies and each team loses 1 token.
  4. Place resources: spend your tokens on any site from your team's device.
  5. Results: damage is applied. Unspent tokens go to the office reserve.

Spending tokens

  • Respond (1): blocks 1 damage at a site hit this round.
  • Resilience (1): adds a purple resilience bar that lowers every future hit at that site by 1 (max 2 bars).
  • Restore (2): raises a site one health level after the round. Lost sites cannot be restored.

Damage

  • Sites go Healthy → Stressed → Degraded → Lost, one level per point of damage.
  • −1 more if two or more of a site's traits are hit at once.
  • −1 more in a heatwave at a Runoff site with no resilience.
  • In a calm season, sites with resilience recover one level.

Winning

If the reef survives every round, the team with the healthiest sites (its own plus Puakō) wins.
